WEBSITE DESIGN AGREEMENT
Between Dana Rowe (the Designer) and Pine Street Books (the Client), total fee USD 4,000.
1. Payment. The Client pays in three installments: USD 1,000 on 15 December 2026 for the wireframes; USD 1,800 on 15 January 2027 for the finished design; USD 1,200 on 15 February 2027 on launch. Invoices are due within 14 days.
2. Revisions. Each milestone includes two rounds of revisions. Further rounds are billed at USD 60 per hour.
3. Ownership. Rights in the final work pass to the Client only when all invoices are paid in full.
4. Late payment. Overdue invoices carry a late fee of 1.5% per month.
5. Cancellation. If the Client cancels, the Client pays for work completed plus a kill fee of 25% of the remaining fee.
